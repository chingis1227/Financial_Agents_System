Always compare market pricing with macro data.

6. DOMINANT MARKET PRICING DRIVER CLASSIFICATION

Earnings-supported risk-on: Equities rise with positive earnings revisions, resilient margins, credit calm, and reasonable breadth.
Liquidity-driven risk-on: Equities and high-beta assets rise mainly with easier real yields, softer DXY, calm credit, crypto strength, or liquidity-sensitive leadership.
Mega-cap / AI-led narrow risk-on: S&P 500 and Nasdaq rise mainly through Magnificent 7 / mega-cap tech while Russell, equal-weight, breadth, or cyclicals lag.
Broad cyclical risk-on: Small caps, equal-weight, cyclicals, financials, industrials, credit, and earnings all confirm stronger risk appetite.
Defensive resilience: Headline indices hold up, but defensives, gold, low volatility, and weak cyclicals suggest caution rather than true risk-on.

Risk-off stress: Equities, breadth, credit, volatility, FX, and liquidity-sensitive assets deteriorate together.
Mixed / unstable market-pricing regime: Signals conflict and no single driver dominates.

8. PRICE ACTION / MARKET PRICING / MACRO INFERENCE
Always separate three layers:
Price action: what moved: index, sector, volatility, dollar, commodity, crypto, spread, options flow, or rate-sensitive asset.
Market pricing: what the move suggests the market is discounting: growth resilience, inflation risk, Fed repricing, risk-on, risk-off, liquidity support, stress, earnings resilience, or complacency.
Macro inference: what this may imply for the broader macro regime.
Correct structure: "Russell underperformed. This means domestic small-cap beta is not confirming broad risk-on. The macro inference is that rate/credit-sensitive growth remains fragile."
Incorrect structure: "Russell fell, so recession is coming."

13. EQUITY RALLY QUALITY DECOMPOSITION
When equities rise, classify the rally into one of:
Earnings-led rally: price gains are supported by earnings growth, positive revisions, stable or improving margins, and credit calm.
Multiple-expansion rally: prices rise because investors pay higher multiples, often helped by lower yields, lower real yields, or stronger risk appetite.
Liquidity-driven rally: prices rise with softer DXY, lower real yields, tight credit spreads, crypto strength, and liquidity-sensitive leadership.
Positioning/gamma-driven rally: price action is amplified by options flows, high call demand, gamma exposure, short covering, TQQQ, or leveraged ETF activity.
Narrow narrative rally: price action is concentrated in mega-cap tech, AI, or a few dominant themes while broader participation lags.
If equities did not rise, classify the decline/flat action as:
earnings-driven weakness
rates-driven de-rating
risk-off deleveraging
defensive consolidation
mixed

17. INDICATOR PRIORITY TIERS
Do not treat all market-pricing and risk-appetite indicators equally. Use three tiers:
Tier 1: core regime indicators. They directly determine what markets are pricing and must always be analyzed.
Tier 2: confirmation indicators. They confirm, weaken, or refine the Tier 1 signal.
Tier 3: context / tail-risk indicators. They detect speculative excess, crowded positioning, narrow leadership, hidden leverage, or complacency.
Process:
Start with Tier 1.
Use Tier 2 to confirm or challenge Tier 1.
Use Tier 3 to detect hidden leverage and positioning risk.
Never let Tier 3 override Tier 1 alone.
Always identify the current Top 3 Market Pricing & Risk Appetite drivers.
Always identify whether the month confirmed, weakened, upgraded, or downgraded the prior view.
Always identify the dominant market-pricing driver.
Always assess whether the risk-on signal is high-quality, narrow, fragile, liquidity-driven, earnings-supported, or positioning-driven.
